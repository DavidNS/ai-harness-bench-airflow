Dags can now be drained: stop new scheduled runs while the runs in flight finish

A Dag now has a ``scheduling_state`` of ``active``, ``draining`` or ``paused``. While a Dag is ``draining`` the
scheduler creates no new scheduled or asset-triggered runs but keeps scheduling the task instances of its queued
and running runs, and it pauses the Dag by itself once those runs have finished. Manual triggers and backfills
remain available.

The REST API ``PATCH /dags/{dag_id}`` and ``PATCH /dags`` endpoints accept either ``is_paused`` or
``scheduling_state``, and the Dag responses include ``scheduling_state``. ``is_paused`` keeps its meaning, so
existing clients continue to work unchanged. ``airflow dags pause`` and ``airflow dags unpause`` end a drain.
